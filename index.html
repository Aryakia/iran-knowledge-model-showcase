Public Research Showcase

Iran IQ & Knowledge Through Time

An exploratory historical System Dynamics model examining how knowledge capital and realized societal cognitive capability may evolve through interacting educational, institutional, health, trade, migration, conflict, and cultural forces.

Interpretation note: this is a model, not a historical IQ dataset. Historical values are proxy-based reconstructions and scenario assumptions, not measurements of inherited intelligence or ethnic ability.
Research question

How does knowledge accumulate—or get destroyed—over centuries?

The model treats knowledge as a path-dependent stock influenced by institutions, education, population health, trade, migration, war, political stability, and cultural disruption.

Why System Dynamics

Long history is a feedback problem.

Changes in education, institutions, migration, health, conflict, and knowledge production can reinforce one another across generations. System Dynamics provides a way to represent those mechanisms explicitly.

Model components

What the prototype contains

Long-run timeline

An approximately 2,700-year historical structure spanning multiple political periods.

Knowledge stocks

Coupled representations of knowledge capital and realized societal cognitive capability.

Historical events

Major disruptions, transitions, and contextual events represented as part of the modeled trajectory.

Scholar profiles

Historical scholars provide an additional evidence and interpretation layer around knowledge production.

Conflict mechanism

An asymmetric invasion mechanism distinguishes chronic conflict from catastrophic state breach.

Transparent assumptions

The working model documents equations, parameters, evidence classes, and limitations rather than hiding uncertainty.

Research status

Exploratory, not calibrated.

The model is intended for hypothesis development and scenario comparison. It should not be used for historical ranking or causal estimation.
