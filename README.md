# Iran IQ & Knowledge Through Time — Public Showcase

**An exploratory historical System Dynamics model of knowledge capital and realized societal cognitive capability across roughly 2,700 years of Iranian history.**

**Repository type:** Public project showcase  
**Project area:** System Dynamics · historical modeling · knowledge systems  
**Canonical working model:** Private  
**Status:** Exploratory research prototype

---

## Important interpretation note

**This project is a model, not a historical IQ dataset.**

Direct national IQ observations do not exist for most of the historical timeline. Historical index values in the prototype are proxy-based reconstructions and scenario assumptions. They are not measurements of inherited intelligence or claims about ethnic ability.

## Project overview

The model explores how knowledge and realized societal cognitive capability may change through interacting forces such as education, health, institutions, trade, geography, migration, war, political stability, and cultural destruction.

## What I built

- Approximately 2,700-year historical timeline
- Modeled political periods and major historical events
- Historical scholar profiles
- Coupled stocks for knowledge capital and realized cognitive capability
- An asymmetric invasion mechanism separating chronic conflict from catastrophic state breach
- Transparent equations, parameters, evidence classes, and limitations in the working model

## Why System Dynamics

Knowledge accumulation is path-dependent. Education, institutions, migration, destruction, trade, population health, and political conditions can reinforce or erode one another across generations. System Dynamics provides a way to make those hypotheses explicit and test their implications over long historical horizons.

## Research status

The model is exploratory and uncalibrated. It supports scenario comparison and hypothesis development, not historical ranking or causal estimation.

## Repository note

This repository is a curated public showcase. Detailed equations, model implementation, datasets, calibration work, and working assumptions remain in a private research repository.
